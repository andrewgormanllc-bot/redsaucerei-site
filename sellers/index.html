Seller Questions, Answered

The things sellers ask us first.

What does "selling on terms" actually mean?
Instead of one lump sum at a discount, you get your price paid over an agreed schedule — for many sellers that means a healthy amount at closing plus dependable monthly payments after. It's how we can pay full price when cash buyers can't: the structure does the work the discount would have done.
Is that safe for me as the seller?
The deal closes professionally through title and escrow, and what we owe you is secured against the property and recorded — the same machinery banks use when they lend. And we'll always encourage you to have your own attorney or advisor look over the structure before you sign. Straight answers only; if a deal isn't right for you, we'd rather tell you.
Do I pay any fees or commissions?
Working with us costs you nothing. Selling direct means no agent commissions at all. If your property is listed with an agent, their commission is protected inside our offer — we never ask an agent to give up their side.
What if I still have a mortgage on the property?
Very common, and often still workable — there are structures designed exactly for this. It depends on the numbers, so tell us the situation honestly and we'll tell you honestly whether we can make it work.
Do you buy houses in bad condition?
Yes — as-is means exactly that. Fire-damaged to fully renovated, tenant-occupied to long vacant. Condition affects the structure, not our interest.
How fast will I hear back?
Days, not weeks. Our underwriting runs before we respond, so when we come back it's with real options, not a "maybe".
