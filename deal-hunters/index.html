Hunter Questions, Answered

The things future hunters ask first.

Does it cost anything to join?
No. Nothing to buy, no course fees, no subscription. We earn on deals; you earn on deals you source. Aligned incentives — the way it should be.
Do I need a license, experience, or money?
None of the three. The engine does the maths, the team does the deals, and the training fills in everything else. You bring effort and eyes.
How exactly do I get paid?
When a deal you sourced closes, you're compensated at closing — handled professionally through the closing process, so it's clean and documented every time.
How much time do I need?
It flexes around your life. Plenty of hunters spot properties during their normal week and submit them in minutes. More time in = more shots on goal, but there's no quota and no clock.
What's a "birddog"?
Old real-estate slang for exactly this role: the person who finds the opportunity and points the team at it. We call them Deal Hunters, because that's what it is — hunting deals.
I'm already on the team — where's my guide?
Your Start Guide is at redsaucerei.com/start — first week, step by step.
