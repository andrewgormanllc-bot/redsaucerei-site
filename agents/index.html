Agent Questions, Answered

The things agents ask us first.

Is my commission really safe on a seller-finance deal?
Yes — and this is the question that matters most, so here's the straight answer: your commission is written into the purchase structure on every offer we make. It's typically paid at closing through title, exactly like a conventional deal. If a structure can't accommodate your full commission, we don't present that structure.
What does "buying on terms" actually mean?
Instead of one lump sum at a discount, the seller gets their price paid over time — seller financing, an installment structure, or another arrangement built around their situation. For the right seller (no urgent need for every dollar on day one, wants their full number, or wants monthly income), terms often beat any cash offer they'll ever see.
How fast will you give me an answer?
Days, not weeks. Every property goes through our underwriting engine before we respond, so when we come back to you it's a real answer: an offer, a no, or a short list of what we'd need to know. We don't sit on listings and we don't ghost agents.
What kinds of properties do you buy?
Single-family, small multi-family, and portfolios — on-market or off. Long days-on-market and hard-to-finance properties are our specialty, not our exception.
Do you actually close?
We only make offers our underwriting supports, which is exactly why we close the ones we make. We'd rather pass quickly than tie up your listing — your time and your seller's trust are worth more than a maybe.
